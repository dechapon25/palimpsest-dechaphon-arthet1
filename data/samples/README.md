The LICENSE file at the repository root applies to source code and

digitisations, included for demonstration and research purposes.

## Underlying documents

The manuscripts themselves date from the 15th to the 19th century and are
long out of copyright as works of authorship.

The **digital reproductions** are a separate matter: they were produced by
the holding institutions, which publish their own conditions of use.
Anyone reusing these files — particularly for commercial purposes — should
consult those conditions directly rather than relying on this file.

## Per-file provenance

All sample images come from PARES. Each file carries the source watermark
*"Archivos Estatales, https://pares.cultura.gob.es"* along the bottom edge,
together with the Archivos Estatales logo, so attribution travels with the
image itself. Several also show the holding archive's stamps (for example
A.H.N. Estado) and a handwritten catalogue reference.

Filenames are local to this repository — they were assigned during
development and are not PARES catalogue identifiers. The authoritative
reference for each page is the one visible in the scan.

| File | Holding archive | Reference on scan | Content |
|---|---|---|---|

Primary demo document. |
| `pares_easy_18c.jpg` | A.H.N. Estado | Estado, legajo 84, N.3 | Letter from Rota, 29 January 1810, to Pedro de Rivero, acknowledging orders from the Suprema Junta de Gobierno de España e Indias. Legible baseline case. |

**Note on filenames.** The century suffixes were assigned during development
and do not all match the documents. `pares_easy_18c.jpg` and
`pares_margins_18c.jpg` are both from 1810, and the `colon_1498_15c.jpg` sheet
describes 15th-century events but is itself a later archival descriptor in a
much more recent hand. The dates above come from the scans themselves.

## PARES

Portal de Archivos Españoles, Ministerio de Cultura (Spain).
https://pares.cultura.gob.es

PARES publishes its own terms of use. **Those terms have not yet been
verified against this repository's redistribution of the files above.**
Until that is done, treat these images as reproduced for research and
demonstration only, with attribution to PARES, and not as freely
redistributable assets.

If the terms turn out to disallow redistribution, the intended remedy is
to remove the images from version control and replace them either with a
fetch script or with scans from unambiguously open sources such as
Wikimedia Commons or the Internet Archive.
